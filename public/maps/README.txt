Nigeria states map files (36 states + FCT = 37 shapes)

nigeria-states.svg      One <path> per state. id="state-{slug}", plus data-name, data-iso, data-zone. viewBox 0 0 800 650.
nigeria-states.geojson  Same shapes as GeoJSON (WGS84). properties: name, slug, iso (NG-XX), zone.
Slugs match the tracker: abia, adamawa, akwa-ibom, anambra, ... fct (FCT Abuja), ... zamfara.

Source: geoBoundaries (William & Mary geoLab), gbOpen NGA ADM1 simplified file, geoboundaries.org.
Simplified further (about 0.016 degree tolerance) to keep the SVG near 41 KB (about 14 KB gzipped).
